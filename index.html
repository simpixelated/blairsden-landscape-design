Welcome to Blairsden Landscape Design

Landscape design ideas for the Blairsden house.

Overview

We are looking to transform our yard into a low-maintenance, family-friendly outdoor space that feels like a private retreat while supporting a small, native ecosystem.

At a high level, our goals are to significantly reduce lawn (by at least 50%) and replace it with native plantings that are visually appealing, ecologically beneficial, and easy to maintain. The space should support everyday use for our family—relaxing, playing, and spending time outdoors—rather than being optimized for large gatherings.

A typical day in the yard includes having coffee on the deck while watching birds, casual outdoor meals, time spent gardening, and playing with our dog and daughter. In the evenings, we grill, eat outside, and wind down in a hot tub with subtle lighting. We host infrequently and in small groups (typically one additional family, up to ~10 people max), so the design should prioritize comfort and function for daily life over large-scale entertaining.

We want the yard to feel:

  • Private and enclosed
  • Calm and naturalistic
  • Engaging for a child (play, exploration, movement)
  • Supportive of local wildlife (birds, pollinators, small animals)

Key Priorities

  1. Low maintenance (highest priority) We strongly prefer designs that minimize ongoing upkeep, including plant selection, lawn area, and materials.

  2. Functional for everyday family life The space should support relaxing, playing, and outdoor living for our family of three plus a dog.

  3. Native and ecological planting Emphasis on native plants that support pollinators and local wildlife while remaining manageable.

  4. Defined zones with clear purposes The yard should be organized into distinct areas (relaxation, play, dining, etc.) that feel cohesive but purposeful.

Project Details

  • Estimated budget: $10,000–$50,000 (Open to phasing depending on design and prioritization)

  • Timeline: Ideally completed by winter of this year, but we are open to a phased approach extending through fall 2027.

  • Property notes:

    • Approximately 2,300 sq ft (within fence)
    • Full sun exposure most of the day
    • No HOA restrictions

Existing Elements to Retain (Non-Negotiable)

  • All existing trees
  • Existing fence
  • Existing deck (20' x 16'), though it can be extended or modified

Design Intent

Rather than a traditional lawn-focused yard, we are aiming for a more immersive, layered environment that blends:

  • Outdoor living (deck, dining, cooking)
  • Play (for both child and dog)
  • Quiet retreat spaces (reading, relaxing, hot tub)
  • Habitat (native planting, pollinators, birds)

We are open to creative solutions and want to leave room for design expertise, but the final result should balance aesthetics, function, and long-term ease of care.
